# Design Context — TCG Vault

## Users
TCG collectors and hobbyists who want to browse, track, and showcase their card collections. They care about card visuals, clear pricing, and feeling proud of their collection. Secondary audience: casual traders checking market prices.

## Brand Personality
Warm, inviting, collector-first. Three words: **showcase, approachable, vibrant**.

## Aesthetic Direction
**Collector's Showcase** — like opening a premium card album. Card imagery is large and prominent. The interface feels like a community hub, not a cold dashboard. Dark background to make card art pop, with game-specific color accents (Pokemon = yellow/red, One Piece = red/gold). Generous whitespace around cards so they breathe. Playful but not childish.

- **Theme:** Dark mode with warm undertones (not cold blue-black)
- **Colors:** Rich amber/gold as accent (collection value, prices), game-specific colors for badges/filters, dark surfaces that let card art shine
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans for body, bold weights for prices and stats. Avoid generic fonts.
- **Card presentation:** Large card images with subtle hover effects. Cards feel like collectibles, not data rows.
- **Anti-references:** Bloomberg terminal (too cold), generic SaaS dashboards (too boring), kids' websites (too playful)

## Design Principles
1. **Cards are heroes** — card art should be the largest, most prominent element on any page
2. **Warm over cold** — amber/gold tones, warm dark surfaces, never clinical blue-gray
3. **Clear pricing** — prices should be instantly scannable with good contrast and hierarchy
4. **Collector pride** — collection views should feel like a showcase, not a spreadsheet
5. **Game personality** — Pokemon and One Piece should feel distinct through color and badge styling

## Anti-Patterns (from Impeccable audit)
- ❌ No gradient text (solid colors only for text)
- ❌ No pure black/white (tint toward brand hue)
- ❌ No gray-on-colored-bg (use tinted shade instead)
- ❌ No side-stripe borders on cards
- ❌ No nested cards
- ❌ No AI color palette (cyan-on-dark, purple gradients)

## Technical Stack
- Next.js 16 + React 19 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4
- Supabase (auth + future DB)
- Pokemon TCG API, pokemon-card.com (JP), Bandai (One Piece)
- TCG Price Lookup API (condition + graded prices)
- Deployed on Vercel
